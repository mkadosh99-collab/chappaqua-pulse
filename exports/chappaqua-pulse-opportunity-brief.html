Chappaqua Pulse:
The Next Local Essential

What leading regional chamber websites reveal—and where Chappaqua Pulse can build a more useful, modern resident experience.
Team Brief
The opportunity

Don’t copy a chamber site. Combine its business tools with a better local guide.

Chappaqua Pulse already brings events, dining, shopping and an interactive map into one polished, mobile-friendly experience. The next step is turning occasional visits into a weekly habit—and giving local businesses richer ways to participate.

Current advantage

Built for residents first

Compared with traditional member-focused chamber sites, Pulse is faster to browse, more visually engaging and better organized around the question: “What should we do locally this week?”

Six gaps worth closing

1

Weekly newsletter & alerts

A curated “This Weekend” email, category preferences, dining deals and new-business announcements.

2

Richer business profiles

Photos, hours, phone, social links, maps, current offers and “Claim this listing.”

3

Deals beyond dining

A Buy Local hub for retailer and service promotions, seasonal offers and expiration dates.

4

Save, share & calendar tools

Add to Google/Apple/Outlook, save favorites and assemble a personal “My Weekend” itinerary.

5

Real event submission

Replace email with a structured form, image upload, admin approval and ticket/vendor links.

6

Signature event guides

Dedicated destinations for major events with schedules, maps, parking, merchants and sponsors.

Recommended rollout

Now • Retention

Become a weekly habit

  • Newsletter & alerts
  • Add to calendar
  • Save and share events
Next • Utility

Deepen local discovery

  • Detailed profiles
  • Retail and service deals
  • Event approval workflow
Later • Platform

Empower the ecosystem

  • Claim a business
  • Owner self-service
  • Membership & sponsors
