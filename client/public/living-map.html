About this prototype. The map is rendered as inline SVG with no external tile dependencies, so the Chamber owns its visual identity end to end and the file is portable to print, web, and signage. Regional v2: all four commercial clusters of the Chappaqua-Millwood Chamber are projected onto a single canvas — 66 downtown, 14 at Chappaqua Crossing, 7 at Quaker Hill Center, 19 in Millwood, plus 3 outliers (Kittle House, World Cup School, Quaker Hill Tavern). The basemap is a stylized projection — distances and proportions are compressed for readability, not surveyed. Landmark data and story layers are sourced from National Register filings, the New Castle Historical Society, and the Old Chappaqua Historic District boundary.
For developers: Replace the DATA object in the script tag with three Supabase queries: places joined to retailers and landmarks for markers, story_layers for the overlays, and tour_stops joined to places for the walking tour line. The render functions and event handlers stay untouched.
